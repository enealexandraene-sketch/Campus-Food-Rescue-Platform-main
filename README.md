## Project description

The platform we develop is an application for the campus of our university. It exists to reduce the amount of food waste generated by the university's cafeterias and restaurants. There, providers (cafeteria and restaurant managers) can publish and list all their left-over food from the day and students can browse food available from all providers. Students can reserve a portion for free and go pick it up during the provider's pickup window.
