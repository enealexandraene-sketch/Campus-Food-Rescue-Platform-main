# Stakeholders and needs 

| Stakeholder | Category | RE Role | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Students | Primary | Requirement provider, validator | Browse and reserve free surplus food; filter by distance, allergens and pickup time; see allergens before reserving; get notified when a favourite provider posts food or a reservation falls through. Be treated fairly (clear rules on limits and bans). |
| Food providers (cafeterias, campus organizations) | Primary | Requirement provider, validator | Prepare listings in advance (hidden until published); set quantities and pickup windows; see remaining portions and reservations; confirm pickups at the counter; cap pickups per student and handle no-shows. Goal: throw away less food with as little extra work as possible. |
| Maintainers (platform admins) | Secondary | User (admin), operator, requirement provider | Approve and remove providers; receive and handle issue reports; keep the system simple to run, reliable, and compliant with data protection. |
| University | Sponsor | Sponsor & decision-maker | Simplest possible app with low running and maintenance costs; decides where the platform is hosted; reputation: the app must be safe and fair for students. |
| Health ministry | External | Constraint source | Food handed out must meet hygiene and food-safety rules: allergens declared. |
| Food waste reduction NGOs | External | Potential expert / requirement provider | Impact statistics (meals saved, CO₂ avoided); possible partner for promoting the platform. |


**Potential conflicts:**
- The University wants a minimal and cheap app, while students want notifications, a map and favourites.
- Providers want to ban no-shows, while students want fair treatment.
